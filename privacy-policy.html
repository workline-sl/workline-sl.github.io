Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026 · WORKLINE, Sri Lanka

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data WORKLINE collects, why we collect it, and how we protect it. We follow the principles of Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: your mobile phone number and password (the password is stored only in encrypted, hashed form), or your Google sign-in identity if you use it.
  • Profile and listing data you choose to publish: name, phone number, town and district, work skills, experience, fees, photos, descriptions, job details and company name.
  • Verification documents (optional): NIC, selfie or certificates you upload to get a verified badge. These are stored privately and seen only by administrators.
  • "Nearest people": if you use your current location to sort by distance, it is used only on your phone and is never sent to WORKLINE.
  • Calendar ("My secretary"): jobs, meetings, money reminders and any names, phone numbers or places you choose to save there. Only you can see your calendar; it is used only to remind you.
  • Messages: chats you send to other users inside WORKLINE, including photos, price offers, locations you choose to share and voice messages. A voice message can be heard only by the two people in that chat (and by an administrator only if one of them reports the chat), and it is deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Payment records: the plan, amount, date and payment reference for boosts. For card payments, card details are entered on PayHere's secure page; WORKLINE never receives or stores card numbers. For bank transfers, we keep the payment slip you upload.
  • Technical data: basic data needed to run the app, such as phone-notification tokens (only if you switch notifications on), and settings saved on your own device.

2. Why we use it

  • to create and run your account and show your approved listings;
  • to match job requests and jobs with suitable professionals and send you notifications you asked for;
  • to review listings and verification documents, prevent fraud and keep users safe;
  • to process and record boost payments and handle refunds;
  • to answer your questions and complaints.

We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. What other people can see

Approved listings are public: the details you put in your profile or job (for example your name, phone number, town, skills and photos) can be seen by anyone using WORKLINE. Customers who post an "I need someone" request do not have their phone number shown publicly. Verification documents, payment records and private chats are never published.

4. Who we share data with

  • Service providers who run parts of WORKLINE for us: Supabase (secure cloud database and file storage), GitHub Pages (website hosting), Google (optional sign-in and phone notifications) and PayHere (card payments).
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it or to protect users from serious harm or fraud.

5. How we protect data

  • All connections use encrypted HTTPS.
  • Database rules allow each user to change only their own data; administrator accounts use two-step verification and every administrator action is logged.
  • Uploaded photos are re-saved by the app, which removes hidden location (GPS) data.
  • Chats are checked automatically for illegal content, and users can block and report others.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete a listing or your account, it is removed from public view at once. To protect users from fraud and to handle complaints or legal requests, a copy of deleted items is kept in a private archive that only administrators can open, for up to 5 years, and is then permanently deleted. Payment records are kept as long as the law requires.

7. Your rights

You can view, correct or delete your listings at any time in the app, download a copy of your own data from "My account", switch notifications off, and ask us to delete your account. To make a request, contact us using the details above. We reply within 14 days.

8. Children

WORKLINE is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version and its date are always on this page.
