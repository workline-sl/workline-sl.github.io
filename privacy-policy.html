Delete your WORKLINE account

Last updated: 29 October 2026 · WORKLINE, Sri Lanka

This page explains how to delete your WORKLINE account and the data linked to it. WORKLINE is the app WORKLINE – Your Skill, Your Job (Android package io.github.workline_sl.app) and the website https://workline-sl.github.io/workline/.

Delete your account in the app (fastest)

  1. Open WORKLINE and sign in.
  2. Tap My account (මගේ ගිණුම) at the bottom right.
  3. Tap My data (මගේ දත්ත).
  4. Tap Delete my account and data (ගිණුම සහ දත්ත මකන්න) and confirm.

Your account is deleted at once. Before deleting, you can also tap Download my data (දත්ත පිටපතක් බාගන්න) on the same screen to keep a copy of your data.

Ask us to delete your account

If you cannot open the app, send an email to workline.srilanka@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my WORKLINE account” and the phone number or Google email you used to sign in. We will confirm it is your account and delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account, professional profile, job advertisements and “I need someone” requests
  • Your photos, links, messages (including voice messages and the private copies of edited / deleted messages), services and prices, saved searches, calendar entries, applications, notices and saved items
  • Your verification documents and your signature / stamp images

What is kept, and for how long

  • To protect users from fraud and to handle complaints or legal requests, a copy of deleted items is kept in a private archive that only administrators can open, for up to 5 years, and is then permanently deleted.
  • Records of payments are kept for as long as the law requires.
  • Digital bills you created stay on your own phone; their short check record (number, date, total) is deleted with your account.

See our Privacy Policy for full details.
