Last updated: 18 October 2026 · WORKLINE, Sri Lanka

A boost is a digital service that places your approved listing higher in the WORKLINE lists for the paid number of days. It normally starts within 24 hours after the payment is confirmed and the listing is approved.

2. When you get a full refund

  • Before activation: you cancel the boost before it has started.
  • Not activated: the boost was not started within 3 working days of a confirmed payment.
  • Listing not approved: your listing was not approved for reasons that do not break our Terms & Conditions, so the boost could not start.
  • Duplicate or wrong payment: you were charged twice, or charged an amount different from the plan you chose (the extra amount is refunded).
  • Our technical fault: a fault on WORKLINE's side stopped the boost from working for most of the paid period.

3. When we do not give a refund

  • after the boost has started and worked as described (it does not guarantee views, calls or jobs);
  • when a listing or account is removed for breaking our Terms & Conditions (for example fraud or false information);
  • when you delete your own listing or account while the boost is running.

4. How to ask for a refund

  1. Contact us within 7 days of the payment by phone / WhatsApp (+94 76 046 6220) or email (workline.srilanka@gmail.com).
  2. Give your registered phone number, the listing name, the payment date, the amount and the PayHere payment reference (or bank slip).
  3. We reply within 3 working days.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method: card payments made through PayHere are refunded to the same card, and bank transfers to the account they came from. We process approved refunds within 14 working days; your bank may take a few more days to show the money in your account. WORKLINE does not charge any fee for refunds.

6. Questions

If you are not happy with a refund decision, contact us using the details above and we will review it again.
